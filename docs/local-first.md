# Local comes first

Nearby exists because a home is the last place that should have to trust a stranger.

## What we believe

The safest brain for a home is one that lives in the home.

If the model runs on a box in your house, the facts about your house never leave it. There is no company to trust, no connection to lose and nothing to rent. That is where Nearby is going, and it is why we start with hardware you own, sitting beside your router.

## Why not today

A model small enough to run on a home box is not yet good enough at explaining a network and picking the right fix. A model that is good enough needs hardware most homes will not buy.

That gap is closing. It may still take years. We do not think homes should wait unprotected until it does.

## So we built pathways

The box does the watching and the acting, and it always does them at home. Only the thinking can live somewhere else, and you choose where.

1. **No brain.** Nearby by itself. Plain code watches the network, flags what is odd and acts when you tap. Nothing leaves the house.
2. **A sealed brain on NEAR AI Cloud.** The model runs in a sealed room that NEAR attests. Nearby checks that proof every morning, scrambles what it sends so only the model can read it, and sends counts only. Never an address, never a time. This is the closest thing to local we can offer today. A sealed room rests on trust in the chip maker, and sealed rooms have been broken before, so we say "sealed, and checked every morning". We do not say nobody could ever see it.
3. **Your own assistant.** Nearby is a home MCP behind a password. An assistant you already use can connect, look at the network and ask to change it. It cannot change anything itself. It is fast and capable. It is not sealed, so what it reads leaves the house under that assistant's rules. That is your call, which is why the door starts shut.
4. **A local model, when it is ready.** It plugs into the same door. On that day the other pathways become optional.

## What stays the same on every pathway

- The box holds the data. A brain is shown counts and names.
- A brain can suggest. Only your yes changes anything.
- Every change has an undo.
- You can leave one pathway without losing the others.

## Where we are

Built and running in one house: the box, the house phone book, the fixes with undo, the detectors, the sealed letter and questions on NEAR AI Cloud, and the home MCP with its password. An assistant connected to that door can ask for a change. The ask waits on the page until the owner says yes.

Next: letting the NEAR model ask through the same door, a password for each assistant, and actions on the router.

Local is the destination. The pathways are how a home gets protected on the way there.
